About

Hi, call me Sarah! As of fall 2026, I'm pursuing a Master of Divinity degree at Harvard Divinity School.

Before finding my way to divinity school, I earned an M.A. in cognitive psychology from Princeton University, where I was advised by Tania Lombrozo. My research centered around better understanding the cognitive science of explanation and its overlap with causal reasoning. I also frequently collaborated with Sami Yousif, Joshua Knobe, and Frank Keil, all of whom I met while earning a B.A. in philosophy from Yale University.

I hope to eventually bring my background in philosophy and cognitive science to bear on my work at HDS and beyond, and updates about my ongoing interests are available here. For now: I'm interested in realism as discussed across philosophy of science and religion, whether and how ministry and spiritual care can operate in secular spaces, and all sorts of odd questions about metaphysics, textual vs. scriptural analysis, building resilient community, and effective public scholarship. I've also started a substack! Throughout my first year of divinity school, I'm trying to post one traditional and one secular sermon a month.

When I'm not working on sorting through all of the above, you'll find me reading at least four novels at a time, listening to nerdy podcasts, or trying to relive my previous life as a dancer.
